## Research

My research section documents investigations as they develop rather than presenting only polished conclusions.

One current investigation explores the apparent similarity between reported electron-gain measurements for argon and krypton. The project looks at the problem through experimental history, electron scattering, and the mathematical structure of long-range polarization potentials.

The research archive includes chronological research logs as well as formal research notes such as:

**RN-001 — An Elementary Derivation of the Zero-Energy Jost–Volterra Integral Equation for the Attractive r⁻⁴ Polarization Potential**

The research notes are intended to distinguish documented derivations and established results from questions that are still being investigated.
