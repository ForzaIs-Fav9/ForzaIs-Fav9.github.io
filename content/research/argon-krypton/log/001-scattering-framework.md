# Building the Scattering-Theory Framework

Having established the central question, the next challenge was deciding how to answer it.

Many discussions of electron affinity begin by comparing tabulated values or quoting experimental measurements. That approach, however, would only compare numbers. It would not explain why those numbers should exist.

I wanted a physical explanation.

That naturally led to scattering theory.

If an incoming electron interacts differently with argon and krypton, those differences should emerge from the interaction potential itself rather than from isolated numerical values in chemistry tables.

Instead of beginning with electron affinity, I began with electron scattering.

---

## Objective

Construct a theoretical framework capable of describing low-energy electron scattering from noble-gas atoms.

---

## Motivation

A convincing explanation should emerge from physics rather than numerical coincidence.

The investigation therefore shifted toward understanding how an incoming electron experiences the atomic polarization field.

---

## Work Performed

- Studied low-energy electron scattering.
- Investigated long-range polarization interactions.
- Identified the polarization potential as the dominant interaction at large distances.
- Established the zero-energy radial Schrödinger equation as the mathematical starting point.
- Decided to reconstruct every major derivation independently instead of accepting published equations without understanding their origin.

---

## Results

🟢 Identified polarization scattering as the appropriate theoretical framework.

🟢 Established the radial Schrödinger equation governing the investigation.

🟢 Determined that subsequent derivations would begin from first principles.

---

## Research Notes

This session changed the nature of the project.

The goal was no longer simply to explain a chemistry result.

It became an exercise in understanding how fundamental scattering theory connects microscopic interactions with experimentally observed quantities.
