# Literature

One of the goals of this project is to distinguish widely repeated educational claims from conclusions supported by the primary scientific literature.

Rather than relying solely on modern summaries, this investigation prioritizes original experimental papers and major review articles whenever possible.

This page serves as a living bibliography documenting the sources examined during the course of the research.

---

# Primary Literature

## Pritchard (1953)

**Status:** Reviewed

### Findings

🟢 No positive electron affinity assigned to argon.

🟢 Krypton is not discussed.

🟢 Reports that stable negative ions were not experimentally observed.

---

## Berry (1969)

**Status:** Reviewed

### Findings

🟢 No positive electron affinity assigned to argon.

🟢 Krypton is absent from the discussion.

🟢 Argon appears only in unrelated experimental contexts.

---

## Hotop & Lineberger (1975)

**Status:** Reviewed

### Findings

🟢 Argon listed as:

> EA < 0

🟢 Krypton listed as:

> EA < 0

No positive electron affinities are reported.

---

## Hotop & Lineberger (1985)

**Status:** Reviewed

### Findings

🟢 Recommends:

> EA(Ar) < 0

> EA(Kr) < 0

🟢 Discusses metastable excited negative-ion states without assigning a stable ground-state electron affinity.
